Privacy policy

Use of inquiry information

Automaix Co., Ltd. uses the name, company, email address and inquiry details you provide to respond to your inquiry, assist with product selection, provide specifications and quotations, and communicate about related matters.

How the form works

The inquiry form prepares an email draft in your browser. Preparing, copying or saving a draft does not send the entered information to our website server. If you send the draft from your email app, it is delivered to info@automaix.co.jp through your email service.

Input and local storage

The form does not automatically save its contents in cookies or browser storage. Copying text or saving a file occurs on your device at your request. Take care with copied text and saved files when using a shared device.

Company brochure registration

Brochure registration sends your company, name, email, optional phone number and selected language to info@automaix.co.jp for brochure provision and related business contact. Once our mail server accepts the registration, a download permission valid for 10 minutes is issued using an essential cookie containing no personal details.

Access information

This website does not include advertising or analytics scripts. Website hosting and email service providers may process information needed to deliver their services.

Contact point

For inquiries about personal information, please contact Automaix Co., Ltd. (info@automaix.co.jp, +81-50-6864-1858).
