PRODUCT DETAIL

Tsubame-chan SL series

T-SL-50-J / T-SL-60-J

The space-saving SL series offers standard configurations of 40–50 destinations and 1,000 items/hour for T-SL-50-J, and 48–60 destinations and 1,250 items/hour for T-SL-60-J.

The figures are standard specifications stated in the company brochure. Actual throughput depends on parcel dimensions and weight, induction conditions, destination count and line configuration. Equipment dimensions exclude maintenance and working space.

Product features

Tsubame structure

The system comprises the sorter body, scanner, sorting destinations and induction. Induction, scanning, transport and sorting are designed around site operations.

Configurable design

Rail levels, scanner specifications, sorting destinations, induction count and inspection access positions can be configured to suit project requirements.

Refrigerated and frozen-environment options

Refrigerated and frozen-environment specifications are available for consultation. Temperature range and equipment configuration require project-specific confirmation.

Modular design and installation

The modular design supports installation. The brochure’s minimum half-day installation is a guide under specified conditions. Access, assembly scope and commissioning schedules require individual confirmation.

Power supply and product liability insurance

AC100V power and Japanese product liability insurance can be discussed. Supply capacity, connection requirements and insurance coverage must be confirmed for the selected configuration.
