PRODUCT DETAIL

Omakase-kun

T-MX-72-J

A 3D sorter for high-volume parcel and e-commerce operations at large logistics centers. Standard T-MX-72-J specifications provide 72 destinations and throughput of 10,800 items/hour for primary and secondary sorting.

The figures are standard specifications stated in the company brochure. Actual throughput depends on parcel dimensions and weight, induction conditions, destination count and line configuration. Equipment dimensions exclude maintenance and working space.

Product features

High-volume sorting

High-speed transport and multiple destinations support operations handling large parcel volumes.

Primary and secondary sorting

Integration with conveying and induction equipment is planned for logistics-center, parcel and e-commerce workflows.

Choosing a model

Choose according to throughput, available space and operations. Both Omakase and Tsubame support primary and secondary sorting.

The indicative footprint is not a guaranteed value for every model. Layouts must allow for equipment dimensions, workflows, maintenance access and peripheral equipment.

Throughput here follows the model-specific standard specification. The brochure comparison page separately describes Omakase as starting from 10,000 items/hour.

What to confirm before implementation

Please provide minimum and maximum parcel dimensions and weights, required throughput, destination count, available space, power supply, temperature range and existing equipment interfaces. Maintenance scope and installation steps are also confirmed individually.
