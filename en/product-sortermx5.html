SorterMX-5 | S & D models and specifications

Five models selected by throughput and induction method

Semi-automatic induction suits small and medium parcels with stable bases; right-angle induction accommodates varied parcel types. S03 supports manual induction. Standard, crossbar-equipped and side-guard carriages can be selected or customized for the parcels.

Drive & control

Lift travel: 1,900 / 2,900 mm. Integrated servo motors drive lifting, and servo motorized rollers drive sorting. Supports DC 48 V contact or contactless power supply, with infrared communication or leaky coaxial cable.

What to confirm before implementation

Please provide minimum and maximum parcel dimensions and weights, required throughput, destination count, available space, power supply, temperature range and existing equipment interfaces. Maintenance scope and installation steps are also confirmed individually.
