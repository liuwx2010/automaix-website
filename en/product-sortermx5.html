PRODUCT DETAIL

Omakase-kun

S01 / S02 / S03 / D01 / D02

A 3D sorting system that automatically conveys parcels to multi-level outlets for sorting and consolidation by order or destination. Choose from five models to match carriage configuration, induction method and operating requirements.

Actual throughput varies with parcel dimensions and weight, induction conditions, number of destinations, line configuration and other operating conditions.

Ask about this productBack to products →

Product features

Five throughput and carriage configurations

The S series has one carriage per column, while the D series has two. Maximum single-zone throughput ranges from 7,200 to 17,280 items/hour, depending on the model.

Semi-automatic, right-angle and manual induction

S01 and D01 use semi-automatic induction, S02 and D02 use right-angle induction, and S03 uses manual induction, allowing selection to match the operating workflow.

0.1–10 kg parcels and three carriage options

All models handle parcels weighing 0.1–10 kg. Standard, crossbar-equipped and side-guard-equipped carriages can be selected to suit parcel characteristics.

Servo drives and DC48V power supply

An integrated servo motor drives vertical motion, while servo motorized rollers perform sorting. DC48V contact or contactless power and infrared or leaky-coaxial-cable communication are supported.

Drive & control

Lift travel: 1,900 / 2,900 mm. Integrated servo motors drive lifting, and servo motorized rollers drive sorting. Supports DC 48 V contact or contactless power supply, with infrared communication or leaky coaxial cable.

What to confirm before implementation

Please provide minimum and maximum parcel dimensions and weights, required throughput, destination count, available space, power supply, temperature range and existing equipment interfaces. Maintenance scope and installation steps are also confirmed individually.
