PROCESS

From consultation to implementation & operational support

From reviewing current operations and identifying challenges to equipment selection, layout design, manufacturing, installation and operational support, we develop an implementation plan around your processes and operating requirements.

  1. STEP 01

    Initial inquiry

    Tell us about your plans to reduce manual work, ease workloads or improve sorting and conveying. We review the target operations and desired implementation schedule.

  2. STEP 02

    Requirements review & site survey

    We review parcel characteristics, dimensions, weight, hourly volumes, work routes and installation conditions. Site surveys, where needed, establish the scope of automation and interfaces with existing equipment.

  3. STEP 03

    Process analysis & concept planning

    We analyze existing processes and volume data to determine required throughput, sorting destinations and operating methods, then develop a concept aligned with material flow and equipment layout.

  4. STEP 04

    System proposal & quotation

    We propose equipment, sorting outlets, upstream and downstream interfaces, and maintenance space. Specifications, costs and implementation stages are presented to support your evaluation.

  5. STEP 05

    Detailed design, manufacture & installation

    Detailed design, manufacturing and pre-shipment inspection follow the agreed specifications. We review delivery access, work responsibilities and impacts on existing equipment, and coordinate installation and adjustment.

  6. STEP 06

    Commissioning, training & operational support

    Commissioning verifies equipment operation and procedures, followed by guidance on operation and routine inspections. We also welcome enquiries about maintenance, technical support and operational improvement after startup.
