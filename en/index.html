OUR VISION

Our vision

― Japanese quality × Chinese manufacturing × AI technology ―Next-generation smart automation

Automaix develops, designs and manufactures smart logistics and automation equipment. We combine Japan’s long-standing commitment to quality and workplace improvement, including the waste reduction, standardization and continuous improvement exemplified by the Toyota Production System, with China’s manufacturing expertise and capacity and advanced AI technology.

We provide integrated solutions from planning and design through manufacturing, implementation and after-sales support, addressing labor shortages, workload, safety, quality and productivity challenges in logistics and manufacturing. Our goal extends beyond efficiency: we aim to create automation with a human touch, reducing the burden on workers and supporting safety and peace of mind.

IMPLEMENTATION & SUPPORT

From consultation to implementation & operational support

From understanding site challenges to system planning, manufacturing, implementation and operational support, we confirm requirements at each stage and develop the plan together with you.

  1. STEP 01

    Initial inquiry

    Tell us about the equipment you are considering, your site challenges and your preferred implementation timing. Our team will review your needs.

  2. STEP 02

    Consultation & site assessment

    We review parcel dimensions, weight, volumes, sorting destinations and available space to define the scope of automation. A site assessment is arranged where needed.

  3. STEP 03

    Data analysis & concept planning

    We analyze volume fluctuations and current operations to plan throughput and equipment configuration, then review the suitability of the layout and operating procedures.

  4. STEP 04

    System proposal & quotation

    We propose models, sorting destinations and a layout covering upstream and downstream interfaces and maintenance access, and explain specifications, costs and implementation stages.

  5. STEP 05

    Specifications, manufacturing & installation

    Manufacturing and inspection follow the agreed specifications. We coordinate installation after confirming delivery access and the scope of site work.

  6. STEP 06

    Commissioning & operational support

    We conduct commissioning and operation checks and explain operation and inspection procedures. After implementation, we also welcome maintenance, inspection and improvement inquiries.
